VOLUME

How much is present. Three positions along one axis, from near-empty to filled to the edge.

Sparse

Isolated marks, wide intervals, low signal.

Clustered

Marks pool into irregular groups; gaps persist.

Saturated

Field fills; intervals collapse toward zero.

02

DENSITY

How tightly a field is packed. Move the control to feel the shape thicken from spiky to whole.

Thin field

Coverage falls off quickly; most of the area is empty.

Mid field

Coverage trails across half the area before fading.

Packed field

Coverage reaches edge to edge with no fade.

03

VELOCITY

How fast a pattern seems to move. Stripes compress as apparent speed increases.

Still

Wide, even bands; nothing appears to move.

Drift

Bands narrow and tilt; slow directional motion reads.

Surge

Bands compress to a blur; motion overtakes form.

⇆ swipe

04

VARIANCE

How unevenly points fall. From a regular grid to a fractured scatter.

Uniform

Points sit on a regular grid; spacing is predictable.

Scattered

Points drift off-grid; spacing varies within a range.

Fractured

Points cluster and void unpredictably; no grid remains.
